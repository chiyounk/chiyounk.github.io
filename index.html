Research

The language backbone of a VLA model is what makes it capable and what keeps it off the robot. My work asks how much of that backbone can be removed, how the rest can be recovered without a simulator in the loop, and how the resulting policy holds up under the visual and kinematic shifts a real deployment brings. I measure everything twice: on LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus in simulation, and on a physical arm with its own on-board compute.

Current threads: offline distillation for compressed VLAs, video-diffusion re-rendering of recorded demonstrations, and reasoning supervision for sub-billion-parameter action models.

Publications

Recovering Aggressively Pruned Vision-Language-Action Models with Offline Hidden-State Distillation

Chiyoung Kim, Sanghyuk Roy Choi, Minhyeok Lee

arXiv 2609.19579, 2026. Under review at ICRA 2027.

arXiv PDF

Width pruning that keeps the residual stream intact lets a pruned VLA match its teacher's hidden states directly. A 63%-pruned OpenVLA-OFT recovers from 0.8% to 89.7% on LIBERO-Long in about 8 GPU-hours, and the distilled student runs 2.23× faster on a Jetson Thor with 62% less memory.

ReShoot: Generative Visual Domain Randomization of Recorded Robot Demonstrations for Visuomotor Policy Learning

Chiyoung Kim*, Min Sung Choi*, Jinho Ju, Chanhoe Gu, Donghwan Hwang, Wonseok Choi, Woongsun Jeon, Minhyeok Lee (*equal contribution)

arXiv 2609.19661, 2026. Under review at ICRA 2027.

arXiv PDF

An edge-conditioned video diffusion model re-renders recorded demonstrations under new appearances while the recorded actions are reused unchanged. On two physical arms, recolored-object success rises from 0% to 42.9% and 47.5% without collecting a single new demonstration.

CoTinyVLA: Chain-of-Thought Distillation for a Sub-Billion-Parameter Vision-Language-Action Model

Minhyeok Lee, Chiyoung Kim, Chanhoe Gu, Seongrok Kim, Sanghyuk Roy Choi, Donghwan Hwang, Donghun Ryu, Seokhyun Kim

arXiv 2607.25487, 2026.

arXiv PDF Code

A 0.9B-parameter VLA on a Qwen3.5-0.8B backbone, supervised with hierarchical chain-of-thought from a 35B teacher, leads the strongest 7B baselines on all four LIBERO-Plus suites within a 2.25 GiB inference budget.
